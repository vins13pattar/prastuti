# Code of conduct

Prastuti is built for participation across languages, abilities, backgrounds, and levels of technical experience. Project spaces must reflect that purpose.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without condescension.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect names, pronouns, privacy, and cultural context.
- Make space for contributors who communicate in a second language.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that affect technical or commercial decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code. Responses may range from a private warning to a permanent ban, based on impact and pattern of behavior.

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the repository owner through a private contact method available on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links, dates, and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with information shared only when needed to investigate or protect participants.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report.
